End-to-End Flow

Purchase RequisitionDemand is raised for stock.
→
RFQ / Vendor QuoteCollect vendor pricing and terms.
→
Purchase OrderOfficial order sent to vendor.
→
Goods Receipt Note (GRN)Goods are received and posted.
Quality CheckValidate goods on arrival.
→
PutawayMove stock to warehouse/bin.
→
Inventory IncreasedStock becomes available.
→
Sales Order / POSCustomer demand is captured.
→
Dispatch / InvoicePick, pack and invoice.

Functional Test Checkpoints

  • Purchase side: requisition, PO approval, GRN posting, and inventory increase.
  • Inventory side: putaway location updates, available quantity, and status transitions.
  • Sales side: sale creation, pick/pack/dispatch, inventory deduction, invoice/payment.
  • Side effects: inventory ledger rows and accounting entries at each branch.
  • Negative flows: partial GRN, rejected QC, insufficient stock, partial/no payment, sales return.
